Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Odyssey is a trip planning app. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights. We keep things simple — we collect only what's needed to make the app work.

What we collect

When you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your name and email address. This is used solely to create and identify your account.

The trip data you create — itineraries, tickets, expenses, packing lists, notes — is stored in your account and shared only with the people you explicitly invite to each trip.

What we do not collect

  • Location data
  • Contacts or address book
  • Browsing or search history
  • Device identifiers for advertising
  • Any data for tracking you across other apps or websites

Third-party services

Odyssey uses the following third-party services to function:

  • Firebase (Google) — account authentication and cloud storage for your trip data. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Wikipedia API — fetches destination photos shown on the home screen. No personal data is sent.
  • Open-Meteo — fetches current weather for your destination. No personal data is sent.
  • Sign in with Apple / Sign in with Google — used for authentication only.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third party for commercial purposes. Trip data is accessible only to members of each trip, as chosen by you.

Data retention

Your data is retained for as long as you have an active account. If you delete your account, all associated data is permanently deleted from our systems.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at the address below. You can also delete your account directly from the app.

Children

Odyssey is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions or requests regarding your data? Visit our support page to get in touch.
